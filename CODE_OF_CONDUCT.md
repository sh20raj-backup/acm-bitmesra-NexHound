# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NexHound is a community project by ACM BIT Mesra. We aim to make participation welcoming and constructive for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or affiliation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Assume good intent while addressing the impact of words and actions.
- Give feedback about work, not personal characteristics.
- Welcome questions from beginners and explain technical decisions clearly.
- Respect privacy and obtain authorization before testing systems or handling data.
- Credit other people's work and respect licenses.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing, intimidation, unwanted sexual attention, sustained disruption, and sharing private information without consent are not acceptable. Using the project to facilitate unauthorized access, covert surveillance, or harm to others is also unacceptable.

## Maintainer responsibilities

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to keep the community safe and productive. Actions should be proportionate and documented where appropriate.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainers through an appropriate private channel. Do not post sensitive reports publicly in issues. If no private contact is configured, contact the ACM BIT Mesra project maintainers through the organization's official channel.

This policy is inspired by the Contributor Covenant. A future revision may adopt a specific Contributor Covenant version after maintainers review and record that decision.
